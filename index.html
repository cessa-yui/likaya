SIGNATURE 01

THE FITTED

LÍKAYA Signature Baby Tee

Silhouette

Fitted and body-skimming. Clean crew neckline, short fitted sleeves, and a modern shortened wearable length.

Signature branding

Exact official sprout centered on the front chest. Small LÍKAYA wordmark high below the back neckline.

Fabric production target

Premium smooth cotton-stretch single jersey. Target: 95% premium combed cotton / 5% elastane; approximately 200 GSM (sampling window 190–210 GSM). Requires physical validation.

Signature colors

Ivory and Taupe with Espresso branding. Burgundy with Ivory branding.

SIGNATURE 02

THE SCULPTED

LÍKAYA Signature Square-Neck Tank

Silhouette

A refined square neckline, medium-width straps, sculpted body-skimming silhouette, and everyday wearable length.

Signature branding

Exact official sprout centered on the front chest. Small LÍKAYA wordmark high just below the back neckline.

Fabric production target

Premium fine-rib cotton stretch knit. Target: 95% cotton / 5% elastane; 230–250 GSM (sampling exploration approximately 220–260 GSM). Requires physical validation.

Signature colors

Ivory and Dusty Blue with Espresso branding. Mocha with Ivory branding.

SIGNATURE 03

THE RELAXED

LÍKAYA Signature Oversized Tee

Silhouette

Balanced oversized silhouette, dropped shoulders, relaxed sleeves, a slightly boxy structured body, and comfortable length.

Signature branding

Exact official sprout on the wearer's left chest. On the central/middle back: the exact sprout above a narrow column of individually stacked L / Í / K / A / Y / A letters.

Fabric production target

Premium structured heavyweight combed cotton single jersey. Target: 100% premium combed cotton; approximately 240 GSM (sampling range 220–260 GSM). Requires physical validation.

Signature colors

Ivory and muted earthy Dusty Pink with Espresso branding. Espresso with Ivory branding.
